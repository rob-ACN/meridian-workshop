Meridian Components has a working dashboard — but one that has stopped keeping pace with your operations. The Reports module is unreliable, your Tokyo team works in a language that isn't theirs, your IT team can't safely approve changes because there's no test coverage, and your operations team is managing restocking decisions without the tooling to support them. The previous vendor left before these problems were solved.

We are responding to this RFP because the scope is well-matched to our experience: modernizing mid-market operations dashboards built on Vue and Python, instrumenting them with automated test coverage, and extending them with data-driven features. We have done this work before, and we know where underdocumented handoffs tend to hide risk.

Our proposed approach is straightforward:

- **First**, we stabilize. We audit and remediate the Reports module (R1) and establish automated browser test coverage across all critical flows (R3). This is the foundation that makes everything else safe to build.
- **Second**, we document. A current-state architecture overview (R4) ensures Meridian IT has a clear picture of what they own before any new code is added.
- **Third**, we build. The Restocking view (R2) — the capability your operations team has been waiting for — is delivered once the platform is stable and understood.
- **Desired enhancements** (UI refresh, internationalization, dark mode) are scoped as a follow-on phase, priced separately, and sequenced so they do not block the required work.

We propose a **time-and-materials engagement with a not-to-exceed ceiling**, giving Meridian budget predictability while preserving the flexibility appropriate for a codebase that, by the previous vendor's own admission, was left with incomplete documentation.

We understand that timeline confidence and delivery follow-through matter more to Meridian than the lowest bid. Our proposal reflects that — phased milestones, defined exit criteria, and a commitment to leaving documentation that your IT team can actually use.
